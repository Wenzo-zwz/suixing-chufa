Each city and attraction lists its sources. Adapted Wikivoyage material is attributed to **Wikivoyage contributors**; use the source article and its history page to see individual contributors. The travel text has been condensed, rewritten in Chinese, and reorganized for destination discovery. Government and venue sources are used to check facts; their branding and other original content are not included under this data license.

The data license applies to these guide files and their travel text; it does not set a license for the application's code, branding, or any third-party image. Redistribution of this guide text must retain attribution, identify changes, link the license, and share adaptations under the same license.

Coordinates are approximate geographic coordinates for distance filtering, not surveyed points or map-navigation coordinates. A source retrieval/check date does not certify every facility is currently open. No real-time prices, timetables, bookings, availability or weather are included.
